About the olive

The olive tree (Olea europaea) has been cultivated around the Mediterranean for over 6,000 years. Its fruit gives us one of the healthiest fats in the world — rich in monounsaturated fats, vitamin E and polyphenols.

Green and black olives come from the same tree: green ones are picked before they ripen, black ones are left to mature on the branch.

From tree to bottle

  1. Harvest — olives are picked in autumn and early winter.
  2. Wash & crush — leaves are removed and the fruit is crushed into a paste.
  3. Malaxation — the paste is slowly mixed so oil droplets join.
  4. Cold press — oil is separated from water and pulp without heat.
  5. Bottle — the oil rests, then is bottled in dark glass to protect it from light.
